# Career narrative

I am strongest when a problem crosses boundaries. I can move from customer language to product direction, from product direction to a commercial story, and from the story to the systems that make it executable.

I consolidated seven CRM and automation workflows for one client. The work improved follow-up consistency and reduced manual handoffs, although the response-time improvement was estimated rather than formally measured.

I built a product marketing launch operating model that connected customer discovery, positioning, sales enablement, and stakeholder decision-making. The model was useful, but the launch outcome should be verified before turning it into a quantified resume claim.

I have often entered situations where the product, marketing, sales, and operations pieces were not yet connected. I enjoy creating the operating model, not just writing the launch copy.
